### Roommate conversations
#### Questions
- 주로 grocery shopping 어디로 가? : Where do you usually go grocery shopping? ; Do you have a favorite grocery store?
- (Good morning) What are you making?
- 오늘 나가? : Are you heading/going out today?
    - 언제 나가? : When are you heading/going out today?
- 오늘 뭐해? : What are you up to today?
    -> e.g., Not much. I have some work to do, though.
    Same. I'm probably gonna stay home and work.
- (마지막 인삿말) : Alright, See ya Brandon; Alright, I'll let you get back to it.
- 거기 얼마나 멀어? : How far is it?
