#### For journaling, lean on __phrasal verbs__ (end up, stick around, get to, creep in), __hedge words__ (actually, maybe, kind of), and __comparative phrases__ instead of "from the perspective of." Those three habits alone shift writing a long way toward natural.

#### Word choice: Latin/French roots over Anglo-Saxon

English has two vocabularies layered on top of each other. Everyday words (mostly from Old English) feel warm and natural; scholarly synonyms (mostly borrowed from Latin and French centuries ago) feel formal. Academic writing systematically picks the second set.

"skipping overtime" → "refrain from working overtime"
"staying late" → "remain at the office"
"gets to me" → "exert a subconscious influence"
"going home" → "departing"
"a thought keeps creeping in" → "this observation prompts a question"
"what should I do" → "what would constitute an appropriate response"

Notice how refrain, remain, exert, depart, constitute, prompt all feel "elevated" — that's because they came into English through medieval scholarship, not daily life.
