# 📚 Semantic Vocabulary Vault

> [!abstract] 🏛️ 1. Academic & Logic
> Essential vocabulary for explaining causality, designing research, and structuring arguments.
> * **a priori**: derived by logic, without observed facts
> * **analogy**: a comparison between two things
> * **apparatus**: structure, system, equipment, gear
> * **articulate**: to express an idea or feeling fluently and coherently
> * **ascertain**: find out, discover, get/come to know
> * **attribute A to B**: to say or believe that A is the result of B
> * **configuration**: arrangement, layout; (computing) system settings
> * **delineate**: describe or portray something precisely
> * **distill**: extract the essential meaning or most important aspects of
> * **exemplify / typify**: be a typical example of
> * **formulate**: create or devise methodically
> * **homogeneity**: the quality or state of being all the same or of the same kind
> * **hypothesis**: a proposed explanation made on the basis of limited evidence
> * **infallible**: incapable of making mistakes or being wrong
> * **instructive**: informative, useful
> * **intangible**: unable to be touched or grasped; not having physical presence
> * **obfuscate**: obscure, confuse, bewilder
> * **overarching**: comprehensive, all-embracing
> * **plausible**: credible, reasonable, believable
> * **proofread**: read and mark any errors
> * **reconnaissance**: preliminary surveying or research
> * **salient**: important, main, principal, major, chief
> * **substitute**: replacement, deputy, relief
> * **succinct**: briefly and clearly expressed, concise

> [!bug] ⚔️ 2. Conflict, Threat & Limits
> Vocabulary describing system limitations, attacks, and challenging situations to overcome.
> * **aggravate**: make a problem, injury, or offense worse
> * **assail**: make a concerted or violent attack on (related: assailable, unassailable, assailant)
> * **confront**: meet face to face with hostile or argumentative intent
> * **deterrent**: disincentive, dissuasion, damper
> * **deprive A of B**: prevent from having or using something
> * **formidable**: intimidating, forbidding, redoubtable, daunting, frightening; onerous, arduous, taxing, difficult, hard, laborious, burdensome, strenuous
> * **impede**: hinder, obstruct, hamper
> * **insurmountable**: too great to be overcome (e.g., insurmountable odds)
> * **menace**: a person or thing that is likely to cause harm; a threat or danger
> * **odds**: the chances or likelihood of something happening; difficulties
> * **predicament**: a difficult, unpleasant, or embarrassing situation; quandary
> * **thwart**: prevent someone from accomplishing something; frustrate
> * **treacherous**: guilty of or involving betrayal or deception; deceitful, disloyal

> [!info] 🔬 3. Science, Nature & Environment
> Frequently used terms in scientific texts, including biology, geology, and chemistry.
> * **appendage**: a projecting part of an invertebrate or other living organism
> * **dilute**: attenuate, water down, make weaker, diminish, lessen
> * **dissolve**: become incorporated into a liquid so as to form a solution
> * **foliage**: plant leaves collectively
> * **herbivore**: an animal that feeds on plants
> * **infrared**: electromagnetic radiation with wavelengths longer than visible light
> * **invertebrate / vertebrate**: an animal lacking a backbone / an animal possessing a backbone
> * **mountain range that had flattened out over time**: eroded geological formations
> * **osmosis**: a process by which molecules of a solvent tend to pass through a semipermeable membrane
> * **pit**: a natural hole or hollow on a surface
> * **permeate**: pervade, spread through, seep through, soak through
> * **primates**: mammals of an order that includes monkeys, apes, and humans
> * **radiate**: emit, give off, shine, beam
> * **seep**: ooze, trickle, exude, drip
> * **shrub**: bush, woody plant
> * **silt / clog / dike**: fine sand or clay / block or become blocked / a long wall or embankment built to prevent flooding
> * **solution**: a liquid mixture (e.g., dilute solution)
> * **substrate**: an underlying substance or layer
> * **thorn**: a stiff, sharp-pointed woody projection on a plant

> [!tip] 💬 7. Idioms & Conversational Phrases
> Crucial phrases and idiomatic expressions for listening comprehension and everyday conversations.
> * **get the hang of sth**: to learn how to do something, especially when it is not obvious or simple
> * **come to think of it**: on reflection (said when an idea or point occurs to one while speaking)
> * **old hat**: used to refer to something considered uninteresting, predictable, trite, or lacking in freshness
> * **to this day**: up to the present time
> * **by no means**: not at all; certainly not
> * **in effect**: in fact, really, actually
> * **no matter what**: regardless of circumstances
> * **be bound to**: be sure to
> * **seek to find an answer**: to look for or attempt to discover a solution
> * **go over sth**: examine, review
> * **give rise to**: cause or induce to happen
> * **come up with**: produce, supply
> * **bring sth to the fore**: to make something prominent or noticeable
> * **not to mention**: used to introduce an additional fact or point that reinforces the argument
> * **below freezing**: (of temperature) less than 0 degrees Celsius or 32 degrees Fahrenheit
